About RIM STUDIO

RIM STUDIO is a premium automotive destination in Sandton dedicated to alloy wheels, tyres and vehicle detailing. We exist for owners who care about stance, performance and a finish that turns heads.

Our focus is simple: the right wheel for the vehicle, the correct fitment, quality tyres, and professional detailing executed with attention to detail. Whether you drive a performance saloon, a luxury SUV or a bakkie that works hard, we help you achieve a look and feel that matches the standard of the machine.

Located at 183 Rivonia Road, Morningside, our showroom is designed to feel modern, precise and uncompromising — the same atmosphere you experience on this website. Visit us to experience the difference in person.
