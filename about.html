About RIM STUDIO

A Sandton showroom built around fitment.

RIM STUDIO exists because too many wheel upgrades go wrong at the fitment stage. We source premium alloy wheels and tyres, match them precisely to your vehicle, and finish the job the way a performance showroom should.

Our Approach

Wheels are only premium if they fit properly.

We stock forged, monoblock and genuine OEM wheel sets for vehicles including Range Rover, Mercedes G-Class, Audi, Porsche Cayenne and Nissan Skyline GT-R. Every set is checked against bolt pattern, offset and tyre size before it reaches your car — no guesswork, no forcing a fitment that isn't right.

Alongside wheels and tyres, our detailing service brings the rest of the vehicle up to the same standard, so the finish matches the wheels.
