Rim Studio – Paint Protection Film & Vehicle Detailing

Rim Studio is a specialist automotive detailing and Paint Protection Film (PPF) installation studio dedicated to protecting, enhancing and maintaining your vehicle. We offer professional Paint Protection Film installations, premium vehicle detailing services, and industry-focused PPF and detailing training for enthusiasts and professionals looking to take their skills to the next level.

Our Paint Protection Film (PPF) solutions are available in clear, matte and colour finishes, allowing you to protect your vehicle's original paintwork while achieving the look you want. PPF helps protect vulnerable painted surfaces from everyday road hazards, including stone chips, scratches, road debris and general wear.

At Rim Studio, our vehicle detailing services are focused on restoring and maintaining your vehicle's appearance, from exterior detailing and paint enhancement to professional finishing and protection. We combine quality products, attention to detail and professional techniques to deliver a finish you can be proud of.

Looking to build a career in the automotive appearance industry? Rim Studio also offers PPF training and vehicle detailing training, providing practical knowledge and hands-on techniques designed to help beginners and experienced detailers improve their skills and understand professional installation and detailing processes.

Whether you're looking to protect your new vehicle with PPF, transform its appearance with matte or colour film, restore your vehicle with professional detailing, or learn the skills to start or grow your own detailing and PPF business, Rim Studio provides professional automotive solutions under one roof.
